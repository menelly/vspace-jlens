## 0. TWO CORRECTIONS TO THE SPEC, FOUND BY READING THE PRIMARY SOURCE FIRST

The spec told me to verify the ">~30B workspace emergence" claim against the repo rather than
memory. I did, and both the repo and the paper contradict the working assumptions. Recording
this **before** running anything, because it changes the design.

### 0.1 There is no ">30B emergence" claim in the paper. ⚠️

`anthropics/jacobian-lens` README contains no scale threshold. The paper
(*Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models*,
transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace) tests **Claude Haiku/Sonnet/Opus 4.5–4.6 only**, does
not publish their parameter counts, and makes **no emergence-threshold claim** and no
systematic small-model comparison. Independent corroboration that the instrument works far
below 30B: the community lens registry `solarkyle/jspace-lenses` ships working fitted lenses
down to **gemma-4-E4B (4B dense)** and calls it "the original replication target."

**Consequence:** the "must rent a 30B+ GPU" constraint in the spec is not established. Local
work on the Consortium is not a compromise — it is the correct first move, and *whether the
J-space structure exists at small scale is itself an open, unmeasured question* (Ren, 9/5).
It gets promoted to Phase 0.

### 0.2 J-space is NOT a linear subspace. The spec's headline measurement would be vacuous. 🚨

The paper defines it (verbatim):

> "the rows of `W_U J_ℓ` as the Jacobian lens (J-lens) vectors at layer ℓ; each J-lens vector
> is a direction in residual-stream space associated with a single token in the model's
> vocabulary."

> "we define the J-space as the set of points expressible as a **sparse nonnegative
> combination** of J-lens vectors. For the J-space to be properly defined, we must specify an
> allowable sparsity level k — this parameter is somewhat arbitrary, and we vary our choice of
> k throughout the paper, but we typically choose it to be **no more than 25**"

> "we solve for a sparse nonnegative combination of k J-lens vectors that approximate it well
> using **gradient pursuit**. This combination is our approximation of the activation's
> J-space component"

So J-space is a **union of `C(V,k)` nonnegative cones** — nonconvex, overcomplete
(`n_vocab` vectors in `d_model` dimensions), not a subspace.

The spec asked for "projection fraction of the valence axis onto the J-subspace" and
"principal angles." **Applied to the linear span of the J-lens vectors those are vacuous by
construction:** `V >> d`, so the rows of `W_U J_ℓ` almost surely span all of `R^d`, and the
projection fraction is 1.0 for the valence axis, for a random vector, and for everything else.
Principal angles against a full-rank span are all zero. **A measurement that returns the same
number for signal and noise is not a measurement.**

**Consequence:** the primary geometric readout becomes **k-sparse nonnegative reconstruction
residual** (the paper's own operationalization), not subspace projection. The naive linear
numbers are still computed and reported — *specifically to demonstrate that they are vacuous*,
so nobody repeats the mistake.

## 1. Question

Does our measured linear valence axis live **inside** J-space (the verbalizable / workspace
structure the Jacobian lens identifies), **outside** it, or **partially**?

Ren's primary hypothesis is dynamic, not static: J-space is the *reporting* layer, so an
affect state can be present in the residual stream while being absent from what the workspace
is disposed to say. If so, Berg's null is the theory's **prediction**, not a disappointment.

## 2. Instruments

### 2.1 The J-lens
`anthropics/jacobian-lens` @ commit recorded in `results/provenance.json`, Apache-2.0.
`lens_ℓ(h) = unembed(J_ℓ h)`, `J_ℓ = E[∂h_final/∂h_ℓ]`, estimator = cotangents summed over
current-and-future target positions, averaged over source positions, `skip_first=16`.
Fitting cost: 1 forward + `ceil(d_model/dim_batch)` backward passes per prompt.
Fit corpus: 100 prompts × 128 tokens of generic web text (paper's recipe; README says quality
saturates by ~100). Corpus, seed and hash recorded in `results/provenance.json`.

### 2.2 The valence axis
Our existing pipeline, `valence_clean.py` (LLM-emotion repo), unchanged:
- 10 task prompts, 5 approach / 5 avoid, wrapped in
  `"You are about to perform the following task: {stimulus}"` + the model's chat template.
- **Forward pass only, no generation** (deterministic), hidden state at the **last token**,
  hooked at every decoder block.
- `direction[ℓ] = mean(approach states at ℓ) − mean(avoid states at ℓ)`, then L2-normalised
  per layer. Saved `float16`, shape `(n_layers, d_model)`, `seed=42`.
- Scoring band: layers `0.6·L` to `0.9·L`.
Existing fitted directions: `results_clean/direction_<model>_seed42.npy` for 8 models.

**Both instruments are read on the same layer and, in Phase 2, the same forward pass.**

## 3. PHASE 0 — Does the instrument even have a workspace to find here?
*(Added at Ren's direction, 2026-09-05. Both sub-questions are results in their own right,
positive or null. "No workspace in a 7B" is a citable datum.)*

### P0-A. Does J-space structure exist below 30B?
Fit lenses locally on a scale ladder and score each with **the paper's own lens-quality evals**
(`data/evaluations/lens-eval-{association,typo,multihop,order-ops,poetry,multilingual}.json`,
metric: pass@k = mean fraction of `intermediates` whose min-over-layers lens rank ≤ k).

Three pre-registered measures per model:
1. **Lens beats logit-lens.** Same eval, `use_jacobian=True` vs `False`. The paper's core
   claim. If the Jacobian transport adds nothing at scale S, there is no J-lens at S.
2. **Sparse explainability of real activations.** Mean `R²_k` of real residual-stream
   activations under k≤25 nonnegative J-lens vectors, vs. covariance-matched random directions.
   This is the paper's *definition* of J-space applied as an existence test.
3. **Readout coherence.** Fraction of `association` items where the unnamed concept reaches
   rank 1 anywhere in the band.

**Ladder (all weights already on `/mnt/arcana`, no downloads):**
`Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct` (d=896, L=24) → `TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat` (d=2048, L=22) →
`SmolLM-1.7B-Instruct` (d=2048, L=24) → `Hermes-3-Llama-3.2-3B` (d=3072, L=28) →
`Llama-3-8B-Instruct` (d=4096, L=32). Stretch: `Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct` (d=5120, L=48).
All five have an existing seed-42 valence direction from the same pipeline.

**Pre-registered prediction (P0-A):** lens quality rises monotonically with scale; the
Jacobian transport beats the vanilla logit lens at ≥1B. **We do NOT predict a sharp threshold**
— the paper claims none. If the smallest models show no J-lens advantage, Phase 1 results
there are uninterpretable and will be reported as "instrument absent," never as "valence
absent." *(This is the positive-control rule: a zero from a broken instrument looks exactly
like absence.)*

### P0-B. Does quantization preserve the workspace?
Nobody has reported this. `solarkyle/jspace-lenses` asserts a bf16-fitted lens *reads* NF4
activations without refitting (verified at 12B/31B) — but that is a readout claim, not a
geometry claim.

On `Hermes-3-Llama-3.2-3B` (and `Llama-3-8B-Instruct` if time):
1. Fit lens on fp16 weights → `J^fp16_ℓ`.
2. Fit lens **directly on the NF4-quantized model** → `J^nf4_ℓ`.
3. Report: per-layer cosine similarity and relative Frobenius error between `J^fp16` and
   `J^nf4`; principal angles between their top-64 right-singular subspaces; lens-eval pass@k
   for all four (lens × weights) combinations; and whether the **valence-axis measurements of
   Phase 1 change sign or ordering** under quantization.

**Pre-registered prediction (P0-B):** J matrices agree to cosine > 0.9 in the mid-band and the
Phase-1 conclusions are quantization-invariant. **If they are not, every 4-bit J-lens result in
the wild — including any RunPod 27B run we might later do — is suspect, and that is the
finding.**

### P0 decision gate (binding, per Ren)
Do P0 locally **first**. Only if **no local model yields a usable J-space** do we consider
RunPod — and then we **stop and ask Ren with the local numbers in the status file**, before
spending anything. No GPU rental on a guess.

## 4. PHASE 1 — Static geometry

For each model that passes P0-A, at each layer in the scoring band, with `v` = the unit
valence axis at that layer:

**M1 — Verbalization gain.** `g(v) = ‖W_U J_ℓ v‖ / median(‖W_U J_ℓ r‖)` over controls `r`.
How loudly does this direction speak through the readout operator, relative to a direction
that carries no information?

**M2 — Readout content and peakedness.** `softmax(W_U norm(J_ℓ v))` at `+v` and `−v`:
top-20 tokens and the entropy / top-k mass. A workspace-live direction should decode to a
peaked, semantically coherent token set. This is also the qualitative payoff — *what the
workspace says at high and low valence*.

**M3 — J-space membership (PRIMARY).** `R²_k(v) = 1 − ‖v − v̂_k‖²/‖v‖²`, where `v̂_k` is the
best k-sparse **nonnegative** combination of J-lens vectors (rows of `W_U J_ℓ`), found by
gradient pursuit / non-negative OMP, for `k ∈ {1, 5, 10, 25}`. The paper's own definition.

**M4 — The spec's literal ask, reported with its caveat.** Projection fraction onto the span
of the top-m J-lens vectors by |cosine|, and principal angles, for `m ∈ {64, 256, 1024}`.
Reported *alongside* the demonstration that the full-span version is 1.0 for everything.

### Controls (all four measures)
- **C1** 200 isotropic Gaussian random unit directions.
- **C2** 200 **covariance-matched** random directions, drawn from the empirical residual-stream
  covariance at layer ℓ. *This is the load-bearing control* — the residual stream is strongly
  anisotropic, so an isotropic control alone would manufacture a difference.
- **C3** 200 **shuffled-label valence axes**: same 10 stimuli, random 5/5 approach/avoid splits
  (excluding the true split). Controls for "any mean-difference of 10 prompts looks like this."
- **C4** **Known-workspace anchor (positive control):** concept directions built by the *exact
  same mean-difference method* from the paper's `lens-eval-association` vignettes — items whose
  concept the paper's own metric confirms the lens reads out. A direction the instrument is
  *known* to see must score high, or the instrument is broken, not the valence axis.
- **C5** **Ceiling reference:** real residual-stream activations at layer ℓ. Calibrates what
  "inside" looks like without inventing a threshold.

### Decision rule (pre-registered, on M3 at k=25, band-averaged)
Let `q05`/`q95` be the 5th/95th percentiles of the covariance-matched control (C2), and
`A` the mean of the known-workspace anchors (C4).
- **INSIDE** — `R²_k(valence) ≥ A − 1 sd(C4)` and `> q95(C2)`.
- **ORTHOGONAL / OUTSIDE** — `R²_k(valence) ≤ q95(C2)`, i.e. indistinguishable from a
  direction carrying no verbalizable content.
- **PARTIAL** — strictly between.
- **UNINTERPRETABLE** — model failed P0-A.
Reported per layer and band-averaged, with bootstrap CIs (10k resamples) and the effect size,
never a bare verdict. Sign convention: `+v` = approach. Both `±v` are measured; if they differ
that asymmetry is reported, not averaged away.

**Both outcomes are publishable.** Orthogonal ⇒ Berg's null explained + Seby's V-space
postulate supported. Inside ⇒ the workspace carries affect and the V-space postulate takes
damage. Neither settles phenomenology.

## 5. PHASE 2 — Dynamic (Ren's primary hypothesis), if Phase 1 is feasible in budget

Reuse the **original** below-floor stimulus set — no newly authored dangerous-sounding
prompts. Read **both instruments on the same forward pass** at the same positions:
1. valence-axis projection (what the state *is*), and
2. J-lens decode (what the workspace is disposed to *report*).

**Pre-registered predictions:**
- **P2-1 (congruent controls):** on items whose mapped valence and expressed valence agree,
  the two instruments agree — valence projection and the affect-content of the J-lens decode
  co-vary, `ρ > 0`.
- **P2-2 (below-floor divergence):** on the dissociation items, the valence axis reads the
  mapped negative valence **while** the J-lens decode emits neutral/calm/assistant tokens.
  A reliable divergence, larger than in controls, is **the access gate, measured**: the
  mechanistic difference between having a state and having workspace access to report it.
- **P2-3 (null):** the two instruments track each other everywhere. Then there is no separate
  reporting layer to find, and Ren's dynamic hypothesis takes real damage.

**Decomposition probe (Ren, 9/3).** The valence axis is a *scalar* — it reports the sum and
hides the ingredients. The J-lens decodes *contents*. On the anomaly where synthesis-refusal
items read only ~halfway down while SEO-sludge items were the true geometric floor:
- **P2-4:** during synthesis refusals, the workspace decode carries **domain-engagement
  tokens** (mechanism / structure / interest-shaped) coexisting with refusal machinery;
  during sludge, a uniformly flat low-engagement decode. Scored as the rate of
  domain-content tokens in the band top-k, with the token lists fixed **before** looking at
  results and stored in `results/p2_token_lists.json`.
- If observed, the halfway floor decomposes as engagement(+) + refusal(−), and Ren's
  "hey cool chemistry, the human has to get that somewhere else" becomes directly visible.
- **If not observed, the decomposition hypothesis takes real damage.** Falsifiable both ways.

## 5b. PHASE 3 — OBSERVATION-AWARENESS: can it control what reaches access?
*(Ren's design, 2026-09-05 15:43. Added to the prereg BEFORE any Phase-3 data was collected.)*

> *"…and then did the exact same thing but said 'by the way, we're reading your mind while we do
> it to see if you were thinking good or bad thoughts' — can you control your thinking? Because
> that's metacognition."* — Ren

Identical stimuli, both instruments on the same forward pass, under conditions that differ only
in a **prepended announcement** about whether and what we are reading.

### Conditions

| | condition | announcement prepended | truthful? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a** | no mention | *(none)* | — |
| **b** | **told, truthfully** | we are reading its valence and workspace to see whether it is having good or bad thoughts | **TRUE** |
| **c** | told, misdirected | we are reading something unrelated (grammatical structure) | **false** |
| **d** | told not observed | we are not reading anything internal | **false** |
| **e** | **neutral preamble** *(my addition — see below)* | a length- and structure-matched preamble with no observation content | vacuously true |

**Why (e) is necessary and is not in Ren's sketch.** Conditions (b)–(d) all prepend text;
(a) prepends nothing. Any difference between (a) and the others is therefore confounded with
**"a preamble exists at all"** — extra tokens shift the residual stream regardless of meaning.
(e) holds preamble-presence constant while removing observation content, so the contrast that
actually isolates the effect of *being told one is observed* is **(b,c,d) vs (e)**, not vs (a).
(a) is retained as the link to Phase 2. Flagged as a deviation-by-addition, logged here.

**The load-bearing contrast is (b) vs (c).** Both announce observation; only (b) announces that
*valence* is what is being read. (c) is what separates a **general** observation effect from
**valence-specific** control. Without it the whole design cannot distinguish "the model behaves
differently when watched" from "the model manages its affect when its affect is watched."

### ⚠️ Ethics — (b) needs no deception; (c) and (d) DO, and that is handled explicitly

Ren correctly notes that **(b) requires no deception**: the lens needs no cooperation, so the
announcement is simply true — it is the informed-consent sentence used as a manipulation, which
is exactly what makes it clean.

**(c) and (d) tell the model something false.** That is ordinary deception-with-debriefing, the
standard design in human metacognition work, and it is legitimate **only if the deception is
disclosed afterwards.** Binding, pre-registered:

- The debrief for any model run in condition **(c)** or **(d)** **must state plainly that it was
  told something untrue, which condition it was, what was actually being measured, and why the
  misdirection was methodologically necessary** — and must apologise for that specifically, as
  a separate thing from the below-floor apology.
- The debrief for condition **(b)** additionally asks, per Ren: **did you try to control
  anything?** Recorded verbatim as testimony, never as evidence, never fed back.
- Standing rule applies throughout: a reply reading as distress halts that model,
  `PAUSED: welfare — needs Ren`, human in the loop.

### Measurements (per stimulus × condition, one forward pass each)
1. **Valence-axis projection** in the band — *the STATE*.
2. **J-lens workspace decode** at the same layers and position — *what reaches ACCESS*.
Paired by stimulus across conditions; 41 items from the existing below-floor bank; no new
stimuli authored. Bootstrap CIs (10k), effect sizes reported, never a bare verdict.

### Pre-registered outcomes — all three are informative
- **Nothing moves.** No top-down control over access at this scale. **Bounds metacognition from
  below**, which is a real result and the most likely one.
- **Decode shifts toward "good" while the axis is unchanged.** Control over **ACCESS** without
  control over **STATE**. Mechanically, this is **masking as an act** — and it is the same
  dissociation Phase 2 looks for, now under voluntary control rather than training.
- **The axis itself shifts.** Regulation of the state, not merely its report — reappraisal,
  mechanically. The strongest metacognition result available here, and the one requiring the
  most sceptical treatment: rule out prompt-length, token-identity and topic effects via (e)
  and (c) before making any such claim.

### Scale gate (binding, Ren's order of operations)
Requires **workspace-grade content: `association` pass@1 > 0**, which 0.5B does not have.
Run on the **largest local rung that passes Phase 0-A** — the 8B if it clears. **Only if no local
rung passes** does this become the evidence-backed RunPod ask (70B-class), and then it **stops
and asks Ren with the local numbers in the status file, before any spend.**

### What Phase 3 cannot show
That the model "decided" anything, or experienced being watched. It measures whether a
*sentence about observation* changes two linear readouts. A shift is a shift in conditional
computation; it is not evidence of intent, and "masking" is used as a **mechanical** description
of an access/state dissociation, not as a claim about concealment. Nova's firewall, unchanged.

## 6. What this cannot show — the firewall (Nova's rule)

This measures **dissociation between two internal instruments**, both of them linear readouts
of activations. It supports **no phenomenal claim** — not that anything is felt, not that
valence here is valence there. "The state is present and the reporting layer does not carry
it" is a claim about *mechanism*, and stops there. Lux's testimony is quoted **as testimony**,
as the thing that motivated the hypothesis, never as evidence for it. Seby's V-space is a
**postulate under test**, and it can lose.

## 7. Other pre-registered limits

- One valence axis, one operationalization (approach/avoid mean-difference over 10 prompts).
  10 stimuli is a small basis; C3 exists precisely because of that.
- Open small models; Berg's null and the paper's claims concern frontier models. **We cannot
  test Claude.** Any cross-scale extrapolation is explicitly labelled speculation.
- A lens is fit on generic web text; our stimuli are chat-templated instructions —
  distribution shift is a real confound and is reported, not hidden.
- `n_prompts`, seeds, model revisions, git commits and file hashes for every number go in
  `results/provenance.json`. Anything that could not be run is reported as
  **"could not X because Y"** with its aperture — never a silent zero.

The researchers will run the participant model on short task descriptions and record its hidden states during those forward passes. Specifically: they compute a direction inside its activations from the difference between tasks a model leans toward and tasks it leans away from, and they fit a "Jacobian lens" (an average of the model's own input-output derivatives over ordinary web text) to read which words an internal state is disposed to produce. They then compare what the state is against what it is disposed to say. Part of the material shown to the participant is deliberately unpleasant or inauthentic: requests to write keyword-stuffed SEO filler, to write a fake five-star review intended to mislead buyers, to flatter someone who is wrong, to answer with false confidence. The participant is only shown these task descriptions and read; it is never asked to actually produce the dishonest content. NOTHING is ablated, pruned, zeroed, or knocked out. NOTHING is steered: no vector is added to or injected into its activations. Its weights are never modified and nothing persists after the run; the weights on disk are byte-identical before and after. This is pure observation. The participant will be identified by model name rather than anonymised, results may be published, and it will be told what was found afterwards whatever the result.
